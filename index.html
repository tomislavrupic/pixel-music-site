05 / RUACH BF16

A new default.
The same local studio.

Ruach First Wave BF16 is now the default music model. Create, score planning and Emotional Composer share the existing local YuE2 engine. Original remains available, and old takes keep their exact model and settings.

01

Set up once

One installer prepares the shared runtime and Ruach model, with progress, cancellation and clear errors. Existing verified assets are reused.

02

Keep your workflow

Refine, Surprise, lyrics and image inspiration still offer local Qwen or optional ChatGPT. Ruach writes the score and renders music locally using BF16 precision.

03

Keep your sounds

Existing LoRAs and trained styles use Original YuE2. Clear a learned sound to return to Ruach. Lyrics, accepted scores, previous takes and source provenance stay intact.

ChatGPT assistance sends the requested text, lyrics, selected presets and emotional direction to OpenAI; image inspiration also sends the image you choose. Your plan and usage limits apply. Your recordings and training dataset are not attached. Read the privacy details. Real account sign-in requires browser consent; suggestions and musical adherence still need your review.

TASTE brings preferences and compatible LoRA sounds together. Downloadable sounds use one Use action with progress; local training remains experimental. Playback and waveform seeking stay inside each track. Codex and Sirius-compatible hosts can use opt-in local MCP; its image-inspiration tool continues to use local Qwen.

06 / TRAIN YOUR SOUND

Make it
your sound.

01

Train a local style

Add your recordings, review ranges up to six minutes and reserve songs for testing. Train both the music planner and audio decoder on your Mac.

02

Listen before you keep

Compare the original model with your trained style at matched levels. Approve a version you like, then use it in Create.

03

Keep your originals

Your recordings stay untouched. Checkpoints, settings and generated takes stay on your machine.

Training is experimental and requires separately installed local training assets. Existing users should prepare their dataset again for the corrected joint training workflow. No recordings or model weights are included in the app download.
