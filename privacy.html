Privacy, in plain language.

Updated 26 October 2026 for Pixel Music 0.4.5.

Local music and writing

Music generation, playback and experimental training run on your Mac. Projects, recordings, generated takes and training files stay in their configured local folders. Local Qwen writing assistance runs locally.

Optional ChatGPT assistance

If you select ChatGPT and request writing assistance, Pixel Music sends the relevant prompt, lyrics, direction, selected Style / Voice / Taste context and, for image inspiration, the selected image to OpenAI. It does not attach your recordings or training dataset. Your ChatGPT plan and usage limits apply. Sign-in uses your system browser. ChatGPT conversation history and memory are not imported.

Pixel Music requests non-stored, streamed Responses API output. This setting is not a promise about all OpenAI retention practices; OpenAI’s own policies and account terms also apply. There is no silent cloud fallback from local writing.

Connections and local records

Pixel Music stores its own account credentials in protected native Keychain storage, separately from Sirius, ALi Imagine and Codex. Credentials are not written to project files, Python, model settings or logs. Request settings, raw writing replies, validated suggestions and generation provenance are saved in your project for review and recovery.

Sign out in Settings to remove local credentials and request remote revocation. If revocation cannot be confirmed, the app tells you. You can also manage connected access in ChatGPT Settings.

Downloads, updates and integrations

Explicit component installations contact their model/package hosts. Update checks contact the Pixel Music release site when requested. Cover artwork uses an existing local FLUX installation. MCP access and control are opt-in; the existing image-inspiration MCP tool stays local Qwen.

Usage analytics

This release adds no app usage analytics or advertising tracker. Network service providers may retain their own connection logs when you use their services. The website’s own styling and motion script do not add third-party analytics.
